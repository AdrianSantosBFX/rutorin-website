How pricing works.

What does “starting at” mean?

It's the price of the package as described. If your project needs something extra, you'll see the final price in the written proposal, before you pay anything.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% on delivery. The Health Check and monthly plans are paid upfront.

How many revisions are included?

Two revision rounds in every package. If you need more, each extra round is an add-on.

How long does it take?

The timeline is fixed in the proposal and depends on the package. One-section pages are much faster than a full launch kit.

Will I pay for hosting?

Websites run on Cloudflare, which has a free plan: you only pay for your domain, once a year. Bots need a server, and the Bot Care plan already includes it.
