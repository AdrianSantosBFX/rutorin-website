01Who we are and how to accept these terms

Rutorin is the trade name used by Adrian Rollim Santos, a self-employed professional based in Varginha, Minas Gerais, Brazil, who builds websites, bots and Discord servers for content creators, streamers and independent developers.

These terms apply to every service you hire from Rutorin. You accept them when you pay a payment link sent by Rutorin or sign a proposal through Autentique. If a proposal and these terms say different things, the proposal prevails.

02Services and proposal

Every project starts with a written proposal. It sets the scope (what is and isn’t included), the deliverables, the timeline, the price and the payment terms.

  • Only what is listed in the proposal’s scope is part of the project.
  • New requests during the project, such as an extra page or feature, get a separate quote before any work starts.
  • A proposal is valid for 10 calendar days from sending. After that, prices, timelines and schedule availability may be revised.

03Pricing and payment

  • Projects up to US$ 300 (or R$ 1,000): 100% paid upfront, through the payment link.
  • Projects over US$ 300 (or R$ 1,000), or custom work: 50% when the proposal is signed and 50% on delivery.
  • Payments in US dollars go through Stripe (card). Payments in Brazilian reais go through Asaas (Pix, boleto or card).
  • Final publishing on your domain and the handover of files and access happen once the full amount is paid.
  • Domain, paid tools and third-party services are not included in the project price unless the proposal says so, and you pay for them directly.

04Timelines

The proposal’s timeline starts once two things are in: payment (full or deposit) and the complete brief, with copy, images and access.

  • Delays in sending materials or revision feedback move the following dates by the same amount.
  • If the project stalls for lack of feedback from you for more than 30 days, Rutorin may close it. Completed stages are then charged and the rest follows the cancellation section.

05Revisions

  • Every project includes 2 revision rounds.
  • A round is a single list of changes, sent at once. Rutorin applies the whole list and sends back the new version.
  • Changes to copy, colors, images and the order of elements within the approved scope count as revisions. A new page or feature is a scope change.
  • Each extra round costs US$ 29 (or R$ 99).

06Your responsibilities

  • Send materials (copy, images, logos, music) that you have the right to use. You are responsible for that content.
  • Don’t ask for content that is illegal, misleading or against the platforms’ terms (Discord, Twitch, YouTube and others).
  • Keep the project’s accounts, such as domain, Discord server and channels, in your name. Rutorin uses only the access you provide, and only for the project.
  • Reply to deliveries and revisions within the times agreed in the proposal.

07Ownership and portfolio

  • Once paid in full, the final work made for you (design, copy and code specific to your project) is yours.
  • Tools, components and templates that Rutorin already had or reuses across projects remain Rutorin’s. You get a free, perpetual license to use them in the delivered project.
  • Fonts, libraries and other third-party resources follow their creators’ licenses.
  • Rutorin may show the project in its portfolio and social media once it is live. If you’d rather it didn’t, just say so in writing, before or after delivery.

08Monthly plans (Site Care and Bot Care)

  • Plans are billed monthly, as a subscription, from delivery or the agreed date.
  • What each plan includes and its coverage limits are in the service catalog. Larger or more complex projects get a custom quote.
  • External service costs (such as paid hosting or integrations) are agreed separately.
  • You can cancel anytime. Cancellation takes effect from the next billing cycle, with no partial refund for the current month.

09Cancellation and refunds

  • Cooling-off period: if you hired online, you can withdraw within 7 days of hiring and get your payment back, as provided by article 49 of the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code.
  • After 7 days: you can cancel the project anytime. Rutorin refunds the part of the amount paid that covers stages not yet started. Completed or in-progress stages are not refunded.
  • If Rutorin needs to cancel: you get back the amount for undelivered stages.
  • Refunds go back through the same payment method, within 15 days of the request.

10Warranty and support after delivery

If a defect shows up in what was delivered, such as a bug in the code or something that doesn’t work as described in the proposal, let us know within 90 days of delivery. Rutorin fixes it at no cost, in line with the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code.

Changes, new features and problems caused by edits made by other people are not covered. For those, there are the monthly plans or a new quote.

11Limits of liability

  • Rutorin delivers what is in the proposal, but doesn’t guarantee results such as visits, followers, sales or search rankings.
  • Websites and bots depend on third-party services such as Cloudflare, Discord, Twitch and their APIs. Outages or changes on those platforms are not Rutorin’s responsibility. Clients with a monthly plan get the adaptation within what the plan covers.
  • Nothing in these terms limits the rights consumer protection law gives you.

12Communication

Project communication happens in the client’s private channel on the Rutorin Discord and by email at contato@rutorin.com. Decisions about scope, timeline and price count once they are recorded in writing in one of these channels.

13Privacy

How Rutorin handles your data is described in the Privacy Policy and the Data Processing Policy.

14Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated. The date of the last update is at the top of the page. Projects already hired follow the version in force on the acceptance date, unless you agree to the new one.

15Governing law and jurisdiction

These terms are governed by Brazilian law. The courts of Varginha, Minas Gerais, Brazil, have jurisdiction, without prejudice to your right as a consumer to choose the courts of your place of residence.

Questions about these terms: contato@rutorin.com.
