Level 7 · NPC tips

Frequently asked questions.

Didn't find your question? Ask me directly.

My channel is still small. Is it worth it?

It is, and now is the best time. People who arrive now find an organized home, and you won't need to move anyone later. The packages are designed to fit creators who are just starting out.

How long does it take?

It depends on the project, and the timeline is set in writing in the proposal, before you pay anything. A one-page site is much faster than a full website.

Will I have to pay for hosting every month?

In most cases, no. Sites are published on Cloudflare, which has a free plan for this kind of project. You only pay for the domain, once a year. If your project has a server cost (like some bots), it's listed in the proposal.

What if I want to change something after delivery?

The site is yours. Small changes can be arranged anytime, and I'll show you how to update the simple stuff on your own.

I don't know anything about programming. Is that OK?

Totally. You tell me about your audience and goals; the technical side is on me. Everything is explained in plain English.

Do you work with clients outside Brazil?

Yes. All the work happens online and in English, so I work with creators anywhere in the world. Prices are in US dollars.
