01Who is responsible for your data

Rutorin is the trade name of [your full name][, CNPJ 00.000.000/0001-00, if any], based in Varginha, Minas Gerais, Brazil. For the data described in this policy, Rutorin is the controller: the one who decides how and why it is used.

For anything about your data, write to contato@rutorin.com. As a small-scale data processing agent under Brazilian rules (ANPD Resolution No. 2/2022), Rutorin is not required to appoint a data protection officer (DPO). Instead, this email is our communication channel with you and with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

This policy follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, “LGPD”). If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, see also the section on GDPR below.

03Cookies and browser storage

The site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. What exists today:

  • Cloudflare security cookies (such as __cf_bm): strictly necessary and short-lived, used to tell people apart from malicious bots. They don't identify you.
  • Language preference (rutorin-lang, in localStorage): remembers whether you chose Portuguese or English. It stays in your browser only.

You can delete cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time. If we ever use tools that rely on non-essential cookies (such as Google Analytics), this policy will be updated and we will ask for your consent before turning them on.

04Who we share data with

For the site and services to work, some providers process data on our behalf, only for the purposes above:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States): hosting, security, delivering contact form messages to our email, email forwarding and audience metrics.
  • Yahoo (United States): the inbox provider where contact form messages and emails sent to contato@rutorin.com arrive.
  • Payment platform [e.g. PayPal, Wise or Stripe], when you hire us: processes the payment. Rutorin never sees or stores your full card details.
  • Work tools, when part of your project: Figma (design), GitHub (code) and Discord (communication and bots).
  • Public authorities, only when required by law or court order.

External links, such as the Ko-fi shop and the blog's share buttons (X and WhatsApp), lead to sites with their own policies. Nothing is sent to them unless you click.

05International transfers

Some of these providers store data outside Brazil, mainly in the United States. These transfers only happen in the cases allowed by article 33 of the LGPD and the ANPD International Data Transfer Regulation (Resolution No. 19/2024): for example, under data protection contract clauses adopted by the providers, or when the transfer is needed to handle your request or perform our contract with you.

06How we protect your data

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) across the site, two-step verification and unique passwords on work accounts, access limited to what is needed and password-protected computers. The details are in our Data Processing Policy.

If a security incident may cause you relevant risk or harm, we will notify you and the ANPD within the deadlines of the Security Incident Reporting Regulation (ANPD Resolution No. 15/2024).

07Your rights

Under the LGPD (article 18), you can ask at any time for:

  • confirmation that we process your data, and access to it;
  • correction of incomplete, wrong or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed unlawfully;
  • portability of your data to another provider;
  • deletion of data processed based on your consent, and withdrawal of that consent;
  • information about who we share your data with;
  • objection to processing you consider unlawful.

How to ask: write to contato@rutorin.com with the subject “Privacy”. We may ask you to confirm your identity to protect you. We reply in simplified form right away or with a full statement within 15 days (LGPD article 19).

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can also contact the ANPD at gov.br/anpd.

08Visitors from the European Union and the United Kingdom

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, the legal bases in the table correspond to legitimate interests (art. 6(1)(f)), steps prior to a contract and performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b)), and legal obligation (art. 6(1)(c)). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Transfers outside your region rely on the safeguards adopted by each provider.

09Children and teenagers

Rutorin's services are intended for people aged 18 or over. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must hire the services for you. The site does not knowingly collect data from children.

10Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the site or services change. The last update date is shown at the top of the page. Important changes will be highlighted on the site.
